How to play

Concept: Two teams (Green and Purple) compete on a shared 8x8 LED grid. Players control their team's snake by playing direction cards from their phone or computer (Webbrowser).

Every second, one card from the team's pending plays is chosen at random and the snake moves accordingly - More players = more fun, because the chaos of the decisions!!. Eat yellow food to grow and score points.

Win condition: First team to reach 10 points wins, or reduce the opponent snake to zero pixels.
